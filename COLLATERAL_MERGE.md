# Collateral merge: summary for Matthew

Branch `claude/collateral-merge`. Nothing on `main` was changed, and `app.js` is untouched.

## Read this first: the sources the task described are not in this repo

The task was to merge "~395 collateral people from the family book already in this repo, plus relatives named in the obituaries." That material is not here. This repo is only the **built site** (`app.js` plus HTML). There is no README, no CLAUDE.md, no build source and no tests. The evidence index inside `app.js` points to the real sources, but they live in your local build folder (`C:\Users\matth\family-thread-site`), not in Git:

- `books/tellas-about-ourselves-1991.pdf`: the full 209-page book scan. Its genealogy section is almost certainly where the ~395 people are.
- `obituaries/*.txt`: text captures of the Bill, Carole, Doug and Mae obituaries.

So this branch stages **only what the repo itself contains** about those sources:

- the book's birthday/anniversary calendar (`EVENTS`, 228 entries, 190 distinct name strings)
- the book claims (`CLAIMS`)
- the retold obituaries and census captions (`STORIES`, `CLIPPINGS`, `CENSUS`)

Treat this as a first pass, not the full merge. To finish the job, commit the book PDF (or a transcription of its genealogy section) and the obituary text files, and the same build-and-check scripts can be extended.

## Why the people are staged in `collateral/`, not added to `app.js`

`app.js` is generated by your build, and `deploy-github.ps1` pushes a fresh build to `main`. Hand edits to `app.js` would be overwritten by the next deploy, and they would bypass your build's own data files (`ancestry.json`, `parts/profile.js`). So the new people are in separate files that use the **exact `EXTRA` person schema** from `app.js`. Your build can load them when you're ready.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `collateral/people.json` | 165 person records in the `EXTRA` schema (`id, gen:null, name, born, place, died, line, source, father, mother, sex`) |
| `collateral/notes.json` | One Community-Notes-style fact-check note per person, in the `CLAIMS` style: the source passage, a `verdict` (CLAIMS vocabulary), a `confidence` (confirmed / likely / uncertain), any `conflict`, and a `possiblyLiving` flag. Also a `dedupe` log. |
| `collateral/relationships.json` | 32 spouse links (the person schema has no spouse field) and 17 *suggested* parent links that no passage states |
| `collateral/build.py` | Regenerates the three files from `app.js`. Run it with `python3 collateral/build.py` |
| `collateral/validate.py` | Checks schema, ids, references, date precision, ages and contact details. Run it with `python3 collateral/validate.py` |

## Counts

- **165 people staged.** By confidence: 5 confirmed, 37 likely, 123 uncertain.
- **29 parent links set** in `father`/`mother`, only where a source passage names the parent.
  - Examples: "John's son Jerry," "inf of John & Sophia Tellas II," the three WWI sons, Allie's six children.
  - The other 17 plausible parent links (by surname, a "II" suffix or dates) are left **unset** and listed as `parent-suggested`.
- **8 duplicates merged into existing people**, not added again: Charles, William McKinley, John I, Margaret Godert, Margaret Weurigh, Lena, Sarah Chillas, and Johann Godert (the calendar's "John E Godert").
- **Calendar entries merged within the new set:**
  - births listed under married names: "Anna Thackery" = Anna (Tellas) Thackery; "Mabel Tellas" = Mabel (Black) Tellas
  - OCR variants: "Barnet fl" / "Barnet H" Falderman; "Margaret M" / "Margaret May" Tellas
  - Benjamin / Benjamin F. Tellas (uncertain)
- **Calendar coverage:** every one of the 190 calendar names is either added, matched to an existing person, or used as a relationship source. The one exception is deliberately left out (see below).
- **Dates:** new records carry the **year only**. Each note quotes the full calendar date. Where the calendar overlaps people already verified in the tree, its day is often off by one or two. One date has a full day because the Adjutant General report confirms it independently: George Godert, 27 May 1864.

## Conflicts that need your judgment

1. **Ray Tellas's death:** the calendar says 3 Aug 1940; the book claim and the racing source say 11 Aug 1940. The calendar has a suspicious cluster of "3 August" dates.
2. **George F. Tellas (b. Oct 1896)** can't be Charlie and Sarah's son, because William McKinley was born to them on 1 Jan 1897. Whose son is he: Jerry's or John II's?
3. **Allie's children:**
   - The story lists Marjorie first, but the calendar years put Mildred (1924) and Shirley (1926) before Marjorie (1927).
   - The clipping calls Shirley "the third" child, which only works if you count a June 1921 infant.
   - That infant's birth is two days before the 22 June 1921 wedding date in the book.
4. **John I and Margaret's children:**
   - The story says eight children. The calendar has nine plausible Tellas-born names from 1866–1884: Jerry, Charles, John II, Dora, Annie, Alice, Margaret May, William T. and Ida F.
   - So one of those (Ida F.?) belongs elsewhere.
   - Dora, Alice, Margaret May and William T. are left unlinked until the genealogy section confirms them.
5. **Alice Tellas Swango:** the calendar's 1878 birth and November 1892 marriage would make her 14.
6. **Annie Tellas's marriages:** "Charles & Anna Bailey" (1893) and "Frank & Anna Falderman" (1901). Is she the bride in both? Her death is recorded as "Bailey/Falderman."
7. **Fred Godert's death:** 28 Dec 1862 in the calendar, 31 Dec 1862 (Stones River) in the book claim.
8. **William E. Teke's death:** 18 June (calendar) against 20 June 1898 (book claim).
9. **Mary Godert Johnson:** has two marriage entries (1849 and 1869). The 1849 one is probably her birth.
10. **Dorothy M. Tellas:** has a 1918 marriage entry three years after her 1915 birth.
11. **Calendar errors in people already in the tree:** the calendar's days disagree with the tree for Charles, Sarah, Lena, Margaret Godert and Johann Godert. See `dedupe` in `notes.json`. I changed nothing in the tree.
12. **"n." entries** ("n. William & Allie (Thackery) Tellas," etc.) are read as a garbled "inf." (infant). Please confirm against the book.

## Living people and privacy (this repo is public)

- **No contact details anywhere.** No addresses, phone numbers or emails were found in the sources, and none were added. `validate.py` fails if any email, phone or street-address pattern appears.
- **55 people are flagged `possiblyLiving`:** born 1920 or later with no death date, or presumed living. For them the records carry name and birth year only, with no place and no story. Those names and years are already public in the shipped `app.js` calendar.
- **Two people are marked `(living)`:** the other Matthew Tellas and his father Charles Frank Tellas. They are recorded by name only, as the book claim already does. The claim's tentative link of Charles Frank to a 1990 patent is not repeated.
- **Janice Fratzke** (Bill's sister, from the 1950 census) is entered without a birth date or married name.
- **Left out entirely:** the calendar couple "C Matthew & Deborah Collins" (1987). They are almost certainly living and have no stated tie to any branch.
- **Not available to check:** the obituary survivor lists themselves, which name most of the living relatives. Those text files aren't in the repo. When they are added, survivors should get names and relationships only, never towns of residence or other details.
