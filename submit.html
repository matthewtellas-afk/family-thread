      The family book

      Tellas, About Ourselves, fact-checked

      In 1991 Terrence Hillman, a great-great-grandson of John and Margaret Tellas, published a 209-page history of their descendants. He built it from courthouses, church books, cemeteries, old newspapers and family albums. It is the backbone of the Tellas side of this page. Every claim is kept as the book tells it, with a fact-check note underneath.

      Genetics

      What they inherited

      Slice size in the chart is expected DNA share: half from each parent, a quarter from each grandparent, and so on. Real inheritance is random around those numbers because of recombination, and past about ten generations a person may carry nothing from a given ancestor at all (a genealogical but not genetic ancestor).
